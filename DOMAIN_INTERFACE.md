# SimpleRidesLLC Domain Interface

## Live experience

[Open simpleridesllc.com](https://simpleridesllc.com/)

The live domain presents the customer side of SimpleRidesLLC as a responsive rental journey. It is included here as proof of product design, front-end implementation, and mobile workflow development.

## Customer journey

1. Browse the available fleet and weekly pricing.
2. Choose a vehicle and review its requirements.
3. Submit trip basics, identity documents, and payment readiness.
4. Wait for owner review and approval before payment, pickup, and keys.

## Public product surfaces

- Fleet selection and vehicle requirements
- Renter verification and application progress
- Wallet and rewards eligibility
- Marketplace and checkout entry points
- Responsive desktop and mobile layouts
- Clear owner-approval checkpoints

## Portfolio and security scope

This showcase documents only the public customer experience. It does not include customer submissions, owner-session screens, registrar or DNS controls, credentials, payment secrets, private business records, or production infrastructure.
